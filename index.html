About OMIX STUDIOS

Small studio.
Big ambition.

OMIX STUDIOS is an independent software studio founded by Vinay Kumar, based in Bengaluru, India. The studio develops Windows and Android products and original games, with a strong focus on practical software and accessible experiences.

Products are shipped through established app stores so users can discover, install and update them through familiar distribution channels.

Original by designOriginal branding, product concepts and game worlds.
Useful firstFeatures are built around clear user problems and practical outcomes.
Privacy consciousProducts are designed to keep data collection to what is actually needed.
